About Us:
Anavi Chatbot Enterprises was founded in 2024 to provide cutting-edge conversational AI and data analysis solutions for enterprise clients worldwide. Our mission is to bridge the gap between human questions and complex corporate data.

Headquarters & Regional Office Locations:
- Global Headquarters: Tech Park Tower 4, Bangalore, Karnataka, India.
- Regional Office (North America): 500 Market Street, San Francisco, CA, USA.
- Regional Office (Europe): 42 Tech Avenue, London, UK. Europe Regional Office is located in London.

Operating Hours & Support Availability:
- Customer Support Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time).
- Technical Emergency Support: 24/7 coverage for enterprise plan subscribers.

Core Product Offerings:
1. Anavi Data Assistant: An intelligent conversational data agent that connects to SQL databases and CSV spreadsheets to provide instant business insights.
2. Anavi RAG Engine: Enterprise Knowledge Base Search powered by local vector databases (ChromaDB) and Google Gemini LLMs.
3. Anavi Smart Analytics: Predictive forecasting and sales trend modeling tool.

Security & Compliance:
- ISO 27001 Certified for Information Security Management.
- GDPR & CCPA compliant data handling procedures.
- All customer vector database embeddings are hosted locally on secure customer infrastructure (ChromaDB local persistence).
