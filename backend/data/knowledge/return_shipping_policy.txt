==================================================
RETURN, REFUND & SHIPPING POLICY
==================================================

1. Standard Return Window:
- Customers may return hardware devices or physical products within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery.
- Products must be returned in their original packaging, unused, and accompanied by proof of purchase / order invoice.

2. Non-Returnable Items:
- Custom software license subscriptions after initial activation.
- Customized enterprise hardware configurations.
- Downloadable digital goods and API tokens.

3. Refund Process:
- Once your returned item is received and inspected at our Bangalore distribution center, we will notify you via email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund.
- Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

4. Domestic & International Shipping Rates:
- Domestic Shipping (India): Free standard shipping on all orders over ₹2,000. Express 2-day delivery is available for ₹150.
- International Shipping: Standard international shipping takes 7-14 business days. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination country and package weight.
- Custom duties and import taxes for international shipments are the responsibility of the recipient.

5. Damaged or Defective Goods:
- If you receive a damaged or defective item, report it to support@anavi-ai.com within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the package and item. Replacements will be shipped at zero extra cost.
